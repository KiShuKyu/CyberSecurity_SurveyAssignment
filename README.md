## About this repository

This repository is the evidence repository for our survey paper, *"The Cybersecurity Warning Paradox: A Review of Warning Fatigue Research, 2006–2024."* It holds the record that the review was actually carried out: the papers we read, how we found them, and how we are citing them.

The paper itself asks whether repeated exposure to cybersecurity warnings makes users safer, or whether it teaches them to ignore genuine threats along with false alarms. Thirty works were reviewed and grouped into three categories: **Warning Fatigue & Habituation**, **Warning Design & UI/UX**, and **False Alarms, Trust Calibration & Risk Communication**.
